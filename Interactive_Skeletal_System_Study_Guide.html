Standard 10 Core Focus: Ligaments vs. Tendons

Crucial distinction tested extensively on state exams:

Ligament

Connects: Bone to Bone across a joint.
Function: Stabilizes articulations, reinforces the fibrous capsule, guides movement, and prevents dislocation or abnormal range of motion.
Injury: A stretch or tear in a ligament is clinically termed a Sprain.

Tendon

Connects: Muscle to Bone (fascia continuation).
Function: Transmits tensile force generated by contracting muscle fibers (pulling insertion toward origin) to move the skeleton.
Injury: An overstretch or tear in a tendon/muscle is clinically termed a Strain.

Activity 11.2: Pathological Curvatures & Developmental Defects Matrix

Disorder Etiology / Mechanism Clinical Hallmark
Herniated Disk Rupture of fibrocartilaginous annulus fibrosus with protrusion of elastic nucleus pulposus. Nerve root compression causing severe radiating radiculopathy/pain.
Osteoarthritis (OA) Degenerative "wear-and-tear" softening and breakdown of articular hyaline cartilage. Asymmetric joint pain, morning stiffness lasting <30 min, crepitus, bone spurs.
Rheumatoid Arthritis (RA) Autoimmune attack on synovial membrane causing inflammatory pannus. Symmetric joint destruction, debilitating deformity, and bony ankylosis (joint fusion).
Osteoporosis Rate of osteoclast bone resorption outpaces osteoblast bone deposition. Marked loss of bone mineral density, porous trabeculae, high fracture risk.
Scoliosis Abnormal lateral curvature of the vertebral column with uneven hips or shoulders. Most commonly diagnosed in adolescent females; Adams forward bend test.
Kyphosis Exaggerated thoracic curvature ("hunchback"). Rounded shoulders; seen in strenuous athletics or postmenopausal vertebral compression fractures.
Lordosis Accentuated lumbar curvature ("swayback"). Marked inward curvature of small of back; seen in pregnancy and heavy abdominal loads.
Spina Bifida Congenital failure of vertebral laminae to unite/fuse during embryonic development. Protrusion of spinal contents; strongly correlated with maternal folic acid deficiency.
